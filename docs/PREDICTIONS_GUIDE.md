## Mathematical Precision Standards
- **KP Sub-Lord Arc Limits**: Sub-lord boundaries computed to the exact second of arc according to Krishnamurti unequal Vimshottari portions.
- **Shadbala Virupas**: Evaluates exact directional kendras, diurnal/nocturnal solar positions, retrogression motions, and aspectual virupas.
- **Avastha Fruition**: Computes percentage (0% to 100%) of planetary promises manifested in practical life.
